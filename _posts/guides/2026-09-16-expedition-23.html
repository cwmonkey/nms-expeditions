**Phase 1:** - 🪟 **Glass:** refine Silicate Powder - 🔨 **Cadmium/Wiring Looms:** install pre-packaged Cadmium/Emeril Drives & dismantle them. Only Indium Drive is needed - 🚪 **Freighter doors stay closed:** turn off multiplayer, keep warping & make sure to not shoot the freighter next encounter - ⛽ **Frigate Fuel:** build a Fleet Command Room/interact with it (or use the one you already had) **Phase 2:** - ✨ **Reality Glitch:** Rendezvous 1 (Iryang, Sode 99/R5) **Phase 3:** - ⚙️ **Scrap dealer:** to the right as you face out of the station, near the ledge > **Derelict Freighter Guide:** > > First, before boarding a Derelict Freighter, TURN OFF MULTIPLAYER. > > To finish a Derelict Freighter you must find the Security Credentials (in pads lying around), Crew Log (at a terminal), Captain's Log (at another terminal), and fabricat the final reward (at the final terminal). > > IF YOU LEAVE before completing the above, you will be bugged and the mission will not complete. > > If you started with multiplayer on, you may get bugged and the mission will not advance (no checkboxes in the lower right corner). > > If you get bugged and the freighter does not finish after finding the above, you will almost certainly need to buy another Emergency Signal Scanner and summon another derelict freighter (go to another system to be safe). - 💰 **Make quick units:** Find a `Salvageable Scrap` planet and dig up the yellow wifi symbols, or scrap interceptors - 🏛️ **Planetary Archive:** buy 7 Commercial Cartographic Data (15 nanites @ Rendezvous 3), use them without leaving the station until one is found - 👽 **Can't learn words:** try a Gek system - 💿 **Platinum:** Make a freighter refiner, refine gold+silver - 🌌 **Settlement already owned:** change systems & use another Settlement Chart. To avoid more player owned Settlements, ask Polo for Black Hole coordinates and take one - 🧭 **More Navigation Data**: Fly low on planets and do scans until POI's show up. Go to them and use the Waypoint poles (not all have one). Keep an eye out for clusters of red lights. Space stations also have them in small blue/yellow glowing objects, make sure to check the back room. - 🏠 _Frontiers: Start a building in your settlement before you leave_ - 🐛 **Infested planet:** Rendezvous 3 (Qarali-Cusse I, Eblintpa B12) > **Sentinel Pillar Guide:** > > 𓉱 To find a Sentinel Pillar, defeat Sentinel waves until you kill the Walker, it will reveal one on your map OR see below > > 🔫 A trick to make sentinels easier is to **remove** weapons and weapon upgrades except for **just** a bolt caster in a supercharged slot in a C class multitool. Sentinel damage will scale down based on your weapon, and you should overpower them much easier since you have S class shields. > > ⚡ The paralysis mortar can help a lot > > 🤖 To make it even easier you can remove the Bolt Caster and fight them in the Minotaur > > 💎 Open all the glass you loot; those can have Sentinel Pillar maps > > 🪏 Or go to `Salvageable Scrap` planets and dig up yellow wifi symbols. 1/10 chance to get a sentinel map > > 🌀 Or look for player bases near Sentinel Towers in teleporters > > 🕋 Or do a Nexus mission to "Hack a Sentinel Pillar/Nest" **Phase 4:** - 🔥 **45C+ temp animal:** Rendezvous 2 (Etianl-Ivp, Cournt XVI) - 🫟 **Runaway Mould:** Atlantideum + Pugneum in refiner OR use Echo Locators to find Harmonic camps and loot the wheel barrows - 🎵 **Can't find Autophage/interacting with them doesn't work:** turn off multiplayer, restart game. If still broken, keep going to new Dissonant systems and using Echo Locators until you find a camp that works. - 🚀 **Build ship:** Rendezvous 4: Gagqoid-Eymo - 🏃 **"Inventory full" when trying to claim ships:** exit ship **Phase 5:** - 🪐 **Crystallised Helium:** Gas Giants in Purple systems. Says "Gas Giant" on the galaxy map - 🦴 **Fossils:** Rendezvous 1: Iryang - 🏙️ **Beacon:** Finish building a building. Overseer's Office is fastest to upgrade, if it doesn't work, keep doing Overseer tasks and upgrading buildings. Keep the Settlers mission selected in your Log - 🗜️ **Build Corvette:** Rendezvous 4: Gagqoid-Eymo - ♻️ **Industrial waste:** Find planets marked with `Salvageable Scrap` - 🐾 **Can't find Xeno Arena opponent:** Relog - ⚔️ **Xeno Arena too hard:** Fight the guy on the Anomaly **Finishing the Expedition:** - ➜] If you started at the Anomaly: Once you finish all milestones & claim all rewards but BEFORE you click End, put items in the terminal and/or switch your active ship/multitool, then switch to primary save via the pause menu to collect items & copy ship/multitool, then switch back via the terminal. Repeat as needed - 🐣 You can't copy frigates, freighters or send eggs/pets back - ❌ Cargo on ships is NOT copied over
